## Paste everything below this line into Codex in ONE message

You are generating a single, coherent set of 20 cinematic poster plates.
Generate ALL of them in this run. Name each output exactly as given
(`01-seo-A`, `01-seo-B`, `02-authority-A`, …). Every image must obey the
SHARED DNA block; each numbered prompt only adds its unique subject.

### SHARED DNA — applies to every image

- Portrait 3:4, 2048×2732 px, ultra-high detail, cinematic render quality.
- One film, one grade: deep indigo-black shadows (#12102a), electric
  magenta-pink energy (#ec4899 → #f0abfc highlights), a restrained touch of
  cool violet; specular whites only at the hottest points. Subtle anamorphic
  glow, faint film grain, gentle vignette.
- Abstract cinematic 3D — sculptural light, glass, particles, energy fields.
  NO humans, NO faces, NO hands, NO recognizable devices or brand objects.
- **ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, or UI.** The
  website prints its own words over these plates.
- Composition rule: the central third stays CALM and readable (a serif label
  will sit there) — put the detail, energy and structure toward the edges and
  upper/lower thirds. Single clear focal idea per image, no clutter.
- Dark, premium, engineered mood — "an engineering studio shoots a film about
  growth", not a party flyer.

## Judging checklist (Yash first, then Claude)

Score each image ✔/✘ — regenerate only the ✘ with the same prompt + "different
composition, same DNA":

1. **One film?** Sits naturally beside the others — same grade, same darkness.
2. **Clean plate?** Zero letters/numbers/logos anywhere (check corners).
3. **Calm center?** A serif word could sit mid-frame and stay readable.
4. **Theme readable?** A stranger could guess the discipline from the image.
5. **Premium?** No clip-art feel, no neon-casino, no clutter.

Drop the winners (one per theme) into `public/assets/reel/` keeping their
names. Claude wires them onto the reel (extends the site's 7 cards to these
10) and verifies in-scene — plates get judged again in place at the real
card size before we call it done.
